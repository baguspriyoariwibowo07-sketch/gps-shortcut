`main.ts` writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt`.

## The entry

> All twelve satellites watched you follow me into that cornfield and not one of us said a word. We were curious too.

## Why this line

- The satellites are silent witnesses: all twelve of them watched it happen, which makes the GPS's excuse a confession from the whole constellation, not one device blaming its map.
- Nobody warned the driver — the line admits the silence instead of denying the wrong turn, which is the caught-red-handed turn the joke turns on.
- "We were curious too" is the punchline: the detour into the cornfield was not a malfunction, it was shared, smug curiosity.
- Inside the gate at 115 characters: one line of plain text, well within the 1–200 character limit, readable aloud in one breath.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
